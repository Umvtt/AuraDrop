## AuraDrop 0.4.5: move any part, a real X-ray, your own crew

**Move the tank's own parts**
- Click any part of a vehicle, from the turret, gun and cupola down to a road wheel, a toolbox or an armour plate, and drag it somewhere else (hold Shift to move it up and down).
- Fine-tune with sliders: position, rotation and size, or hide it. "Select the whole…" jumps from a small part to the bigger one it belongs to. Moves are saved with the vehicle.

**Crew editor**
- Add up to 8 crew members and choose each person's role, name and height (150 to 200 cm). Seat them in the hull or the turret and nudge their seats.
- They show up as real people in the X-ray (the loader stands), and shots hit each one individually.

**A much better X-ray**
- Ammo is stored where it really goes: sponson racks, floor bins, an autoloader carousel, and separate shells and charges for two-piece ammo. Ammo no longer pokes through the hull.
- Cleaner look: armour as glass with crisp outlines, the running gear as a faint ghost. Point at anything inside to see what it is.

**Tracks, wheels and skirts**
- Side skirts rebuilt: Schürzen hang straight from brackets with cut-away end plates, rubber skirts are ribbed flaps, armoured skirts are bolted on.
- Mudguards now continue the track guards instead of floating in front of the tank.
- Fixed: suspension arms floating away from the wheels, and left-side wheels and idlers spinning backwards while driving.

**Also**
- Angled armour now resists full-calibre shells more realistically (slope effect), so small guns bounce off sloped fronts.
- Sticker outlines on pictures are no longer cut off at the edges.

## AuraDrop 0.4.0: easier editing, duels and more vehicles

**A clearer app**
- New Home screen with tabs: Projects, Templates, Vehicle Workshop and Hangar.
- Every project saves itself as you work and shows up in Projects with a preview. Rename, duplicate or delete them there. Ctrl+S still saves to your .auradrop file if you opened one.
- Simple and Pro modes in the editor: Simple hides rulers, grids and fine-tuning; Pro shows everything.
- A History panel: jump back (or forward) to any change.

**Pictures and text**
- Crop pictures (with ratio presets) and remove backgrounds by colour: click the background, tune tolerance and soft edge.
- Shape masks (circle, star, heart, triangle, speech bubble) and sticker outlines for cut-outs.
- Flip anything (Shift+H / Shift+V) and add mirrored copies across the page; tanks turn to face each other.
- Curved text: arch it or make it smile.
- Tank meme stickers: BIAS!, AMMO RACKED, RICOCHET!, -1 TANK, chibi tanks and more.

**Export**
- Video (MP4 or WebM) and GIF export with all animations, the drifting background and moving 3D tanks.

**Vehicle Workshop**
- Shoot test: click anywhere on a tank to fire any gun at it. Real plate angles, ricochets, penetration and fragments that knock out crew, engine, fuel and ammo racks.
- 3D duels: two tanks face off, aim, fire, reload and trade shots until one is knocked out.
- Hit camera: an X-ray picture of every shot. Save it or put it on your canvas.
- Tank sounds: guns, ricochets, hits, explosions and engines (M to mute).
- Photo mode: six scenes, sun and lens controls, wallpaper-size photos.
- 3D planes (Spitfire, Bf 109, P-51, Zero, Fw 190, Corsair, Stuka, Il-2, MiG-15, Sabre) and cars (eight styles), all fully customisable.
- New tanks: Tiger II, Jagdpanther, T-54, Centurion, Type 10 and Merkava.
- Place parts by hand: armour plates, reactive armour, jerrycans, logs, machine guns, flags and more. Click to place, drag to move, mirror to the other side.
- Random builds for planes and cars too; joke stats on stat cards.

**Fixes**
- Curved turrets no longer break into stray triangles; the Versus template's tanks now really face each other.

## AuraDrop 0.3.0: the Vehicle Workshop

**New: a 3D Vehicle Workshop for tank fans**
- Build your own tank in 3D, or start from 12 real ones: Tiger I, Panther, Panzer IV H, T-34-85, KV-1, IS-2, Sherman, Pershing, Churchill VII, Leopard 2A4, M1A1 Abrams and T-72A (approximate).
- Shape everything: hull shape and size, every armour plate and its angle, turret shape, gun calibre and length, muzzle brake, mantlet, running gear (interleaved, Christie, VVSS bogies, torsion bars, modern), skirts, engine, crew and lots of details.
- Armour view coloured by thickness. Point at a plate to see its effective thickness from where you're looking.
- X-ray view showing the crew, ammo racks, engine, fuel and transmission inside.
- Live stats as you build (penetration, reload, armour, speed, weight) and a battle rating.
- Camouflage (ambush, splinter, winter, digital…), zimmerit, weathering, national markings, numbers and slogans.
- Animated: tracks roll, the turret scans and the gun fires.
- A hangar for your own designs.
- Put tanks on your canvas as live 3D, or as a ready-made stat card. A new "Vehicle view" button action flips between normal, armour and X-ray in Preview.

**Editor upgrades**
- Drag layers to reorder them; select many at once (Shift/Ctrl-click or drag a box).
- Group, align and spread out; right-click menus everywhere.
- Rulers, guides, grid and snapping; zoom at the pointer, space-drag to pan, Alt-drag to duplicate.
- Ctrl+K command palette, F1 shortcut list.
- Recent files, and unsaved work is recovered after a crash.

New templates: Tank card and Versus.
